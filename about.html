About

About us

Preserving Jordan’s heritage, with its communities

Established in 2014, Tarmeem Center for the Preservation of Heritage is a non-profit Limited Liability Company dedicated to preserving Jordan’s rich cultural and natural heritage.

We focus on maximising the social and economic benefits of our projects for nearby communities by offering training programmes and job opportunities in heritage documentation, conservation and restoration, tourism development, and engineering and architecture.

To date we have provided over 150 direct job opportunities, training university students and community members in restoration, conservation and heritage preservation. Many of these individuals have gone on to become professional technicians contributing to our ongoing projects.

Vision & Mission

What drives us

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in the preservation of cultural and natural heritage, ensuring that Jordan’s treasures are safeguarded for future generations while fostering social and economic growth through sustainable heritage projects.

Our Mission

Preserving Jordan’s rich cultural and natural heritage, empowering local communities through training and employment, and promoting sustainable cultural development for future generations.
